Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026.

FoodFronts YTA is a private tool used only by the owner of the FoodFronts YouTube channel. It is not available to other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission it uploads videos to the FoodFronts channel and reads that channel's own YouTube analytics (views, watch time and similar counts).

What it stores

The sign-in token is stored on the owner's own computer or in the owner's private cloud environment. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Analytics are used only to improve the channel's own videos.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services and follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
